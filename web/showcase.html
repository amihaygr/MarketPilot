ENGINEERING JUDGMENT

The architecture is defined as much by its boundaries as its tools.

Compose ≠ Airflow

Permanent services restart under Docker; workflows start and finish under Airflow.

Delivery ≠ exactly once

Kafka and Spark may retry, so MariaDB business keys and idempotent upserts carry correctness.

Fresh ≠ certified

The application can show recent provisional data without pretending it passed the closed-session gate.

Archive ≠ purge

Exports are hashed and restored before any future retention decision; MVP history is never auto-deleted.
